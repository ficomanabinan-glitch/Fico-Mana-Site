1. System overview

After a shoot, raw photos are shared with the client. This system manages 5-photo selection, staff quality review, editor day folders, a 12 working-day turnaround, and final edited delivery.

1
Gallery ready Staff shares the raw Google Drive gallery with the client.
2
Client 5-pick Client submits a Drive folder with 5 chosen raw photos.
3
Review Queue Staff approves or rejects; client is emailed automatically.
4
Editor folders Approved jobs grouped by shoot date for clear daily workload.
5
12 working-day window Starts day after shoot; late approvals still get a full 12 working days.
6
Edited delivery Staff pastes edited Drive link and emails the client.
